Shapes use H × W × C and omit the batch axis. Architecture sizes assume a 299 × 299 RGB input. All convolution diagrams assume groups = 1. Toy values and weights are invented for teaching. Sampled channel planes are labeled. Kernel weight counts exclude bias and BatchNorm parameters.
